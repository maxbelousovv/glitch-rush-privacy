# Glitch Rush Privacy Policy

This policy applies to the Android game Glitch Rush, published by Maksim Belousov. For privacy questions or requests, contact belousovv.max@gmail.com.

**Game data.** Glitch Rush uses your device’s language setting to suggest a display language. The game stores your progress, in-game coins, unlocked skins and colors, and preferences in its local app storage so they are available the next time you play. Glitch Rush does not require an account or use in-app analytics. The game does not send your saves or settings to the developer or share them with third parties.

**Advertising.** The current version of Glitch Rush does not display ads. We plan to add advertising in a future release. Before releasing a version with ads, we will update this policy and the Google Play Data safety disclosure to reflect that version’s data practices. The updated policy will identify the advertising providers and explain what data, if any, is collected, used, or shared for advertising.

**Storage and deletion.** Your saves and preferences remain in the app’s local storage until you clear Glitch Rush’s storage in Android settings or uninstall the game. Depending on your device and backup settings, Android may back up or restore app data.

**Android diagnostics.** If you opt in to sharing usage and diagnostics with Google, Google Play may make technical reports about Glitch Rush, such as crash reports, available to the developer through Android vitals. The developer uses these reports to diagnose problems and improve the game.

**Privacy inquiries.** If you email the developer, your email address and any information you choose to include are used to respond to your request. The developer keeps this correspondence for as long as needed to address the request. You can use the contact address above to request deletion of that correspondence.

**Policy website.** This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. See the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for more information.
